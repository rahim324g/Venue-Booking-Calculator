Terms and conditions

Booking, payment and arrival
  • A deposit of BWP 500, or the amount specified for your booking, is required to secure your dates. Pay within 48 hours of booking. Unpaid reservations may be cancelled and the dates released.
  • Published prices may change. The price confirmed at the time of booking will be honoured.
  • Be ready to provide valid identification before check-in through the agreed contact method. Arrange late check-out in advance. It is subject to availability and additional charges may apply.
Guests, gatherings and use of the property
  • Keep to the confirmed guest count unless management approves a change in writing. Parties and large gatherings require prior approval. Loud music is prohibited at all times. Observe quiet hours from 10:30 pm to 8:00 am.
  • Use the designated outdoor smoking area. Smoking indoors is prohibited. Pets require prior approval. Guests are responsible for pet-related damage or extra cleaning.
  • Keep the property and garden in good condition and report problems promptly. Wash dishes and take out rubbish. Leave towels, linen and other supplied items at the property. Do not damage or remove plants.
  • Laundry is for current guests staying two or three days. Bring your own washing powder and leave outside laundry at home. A BWP 15 charge per item may apply if the laundry rules are breached.
Swimming pool safety
  • Guests use the pool at their own risk. There is no lifeguard on duty. A responsible adult must supervise children at all times.
  • No diving, running or rough play around the pool. Keep glass containers out of the pool area.
Security, damages and responsibility
  • External areas are monitored by CCTV for security. Lock doors and windows, keep the gate secure and take care of personal belongings.
  • Guests are responsible for damage to the property or its contents and for reasonable repair or replacement costs. Lost keys carry a BWP 650 fee. Applicable charges for breaches of the rules remain the guest’s responsibility.
  • The property’s booking terms place responsibility for personal belongings with guests and exclude liability for accidents, injuries and loss of belongings.
  • Management may terminate a stay without refund for serious breaches of the terms, rules or safety requirements. Illegal activity may be reported to the authorities.
  • By booking, guests acknowledge these terms and accept responsibility for applicable charges.
Cancellations and date changes
  • Cancellations made within 24 hours of booking are eligible for a full deposit refund.
  • A cancellation more than 7 days after booking incurs a fee of 50% of the deposit. More than 30 days after booking, the deposit is non-refundable.
  • Requests to change dates are subject to availability and the notice given. No-shows without prior notice forfeit the deposit.
Privacy policy
  • Legae Garden Home may collect contact, identification, booking and payment-related details, website usage information and footage from external CCTV.
  • Information is used to manage bookings, communicate with guests, improve services, protect people and property and meet legal and accounting requirements.
  • Personal information is never sold, rented or leased. It may be shared with booking, payment and other service providers, or with authorities where required.
  • Reasonable measures are used to protect information. Records are retained for as long as necessary for their purpose and applicable requirements.
  • Guests may request access, correction, restriction of processing or deletion where applicable. This policy may be updated. For privacy enquiries email legaegardenhome@gmail.com.
